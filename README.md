# Hamropedia Digital

**[digital.hamropedia.com](https://digital.hamropedia.com)** · Websites and AI for small businesses, explained in plain English.

Hamropedia Digital builds fixed-price websites and practical AI tools for small businesses anywhere in the UK.
It is part of [Hamropedia](https://hamropedia.com).

## What's on the site

- **Websites**: one-page and full websites, Google Business Profile set-up, online bookings and orders,
  small online shops and website care.
- **AI & automation**: AI assistants for websites and WhatsApp, admin automation and ask-your-documents tools,
  with a time-saving calculator.
- **Prices**: fixed prices in pounds, with every running cost explained.
- **Examples**: working examples for made-up businesses (an AI assistant for a cleaning company, a takeaway
  and a barber with online booking), plus real AI projects with live demos: Planck AI, CogniGraph, NeuroArc
  and FruitGuard AI.
- **How it works** and **Questions**: the whole process and the tech words, explained in plain English.
