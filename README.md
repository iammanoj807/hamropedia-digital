# Hamropedia Digital

**[digital.hamropedia.com](https://digital.hamropedia.com)** · Simple, fast websites for small businesses, explained in plain English.

Hamropedia Digital builds fixed-price websites for small businesses anywhere in the UK, and in Nepal.
It is part of [Hamropedia](https://hamropedia.com).

## What's on the site

- **What I do**: websites, Google Business Profile set-up, online bookings and orders, small online shops,
  website care and custom tools for businesses.
- **Prices**: fixed packages in pounds, every running cost explained, and a page in Nepali with prices in rupees.
- **Examples**: working example websites for made-up businesses (a takeaway, a barber with online booking
  and a plumber with an instant price guide), plus hamropedia.com as a real project.
- **How it works** and **Questions**: the whole process and the tech words, explained in plain English.
